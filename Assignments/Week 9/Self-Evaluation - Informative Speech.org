#+title: Self Evaluation Informative Speech
Building on your first self-evaluation, write four paragraphs reflecting on your
second speech. Dive deeper in your analysis, focusing on your growth,
challenges, and next steps, yet make it concise (between 400-500 words). Focus
on your speech content, introduction and conclusion, delivery (language, voice,
body language), and audience engagement. Do not paste the prompts into your
responses.

1. AI use reflection:
   Explain how you ensured that the ideas, structure, and final delivery
   remained your own work while using AI as a support tool rather than a
   substitute for critical thinking.

   Identify the most effective strategy you personally developed for using AI to
   strengthen your speech(es). Reflect on your individual process, challenges,
   and decisions. Your response should center on your own experience and growth,
   not on general advice about AI use.

2. Strengths and Weaknesses: Summarize the feedback you received from your
   professor and classmates after your presentation (including feedback sheets).
   Highlight the strengths they observed as well as the areas they suggested
   could be improved. Then, reflect on their comments by considering how the
   feedback aligns with your own perception of your performance.

3. Progress: Evaluate the goals you established in your first self-assessment.
   In what ways did you work toward achieving those goals? Describe the specific
   improvements you made and identify any challenges that persist.

4. Goals for Growth: Set 1-2 specific goals for your upcoming speech. Explain
   why this goal is important for your continued growth as a speaker and how you
   plan to work toward accomplishing it.
